Endorsed Lockup

Each operating company carries the AN AIR COMPANY endorsement mark beneath its name. This signals platform membership — without replacing local brand identity. Your name stays primary; the AIR lockup signals backing, scale, and shared standards.

Do

  • Place the AN AIR COMPANY lockup directly below the OpCo logo
  • Use approved single-color (black or white) lockup on colored backgrounds
  • Maintain clear space equal to the letter-height of A in AIR on all sides
  • Apply consistently across all customer-facing touchpoints
  • Download files from the AIRlink portal — never recreate the lockup manually

Don't

  • Recreate, resize, or retype the endorsement mark by hand
  • Use the AIR primary wordmark in place of the endorsement lockup
  • Place the AIR logo separately as if it were a standalone brand
  • Alter proportions, colors, or spacing of the combined lockup
  • Apply the lockup over busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast

Field Applications

New Fleet VehiclesRequired

Full OpCo + AIR lockup on all new service vehicles and trailer wraps. White lockup for dark vehicles; color on white.

Business CardsRequired

OpCo logo anchors card front. AN AIR COMPANY lockup in footer. Min. 6pt; never smaller than 80% of OpCo name.

Trade Show SignageRequired

Full-color lockup on booth banners, backdrops, and pop-up displays. AIR standards govern AIR-hosted events.

New Building SignageRequired

Lockup on all new exterior and interior facility signage. Prioritize main entrance, reception, and customer areas.

Proposals & Scope DocsRequired

Include on cover pages and running footers of customer-facing proposals, quotes, and scope documents.

WebsitesRequired

Add endorsement to website footer and About page. Link to aircontrolconcepts.com. Coordinate before homepage redesign.

Digital & Social MediaRequired

Include in LinkedIn, Facebook, and social profile bios. Email signature references AN AIR COMPANY below OpCo name.

Print & Digital CollateralRequired

Apply to all new brochures, sell sheets, spec sheets, capabilities decks, and email marketing templates.

ApparelOptional

At OpCo discretion. AN AIR COMPANY may appear on sleeve or collar as secondary mark. OpCo name remains primary.
